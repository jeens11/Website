CPSC 581

A Family of Buttons
Four-Part Harmony

Four teammates become a band. Tapping brings a person on stage; personality shapes their musical behavior, while proximity and timing reveal how the group connects.

We chose music because it can express both individual character and relationships. Activating teammates makes their rhythms interact; moving them closer changes how strongly those rhythms influence each other.

My design thread: My event-work identity appeared in an early ticket concept and in the final character role: percussion and crowd sound.
