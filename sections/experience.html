What it taught me as a designer

Observe

Confusion shows up before complaints.

A guest who pauses at a door is giving feedback. I look for the same pauses in walkthroughs.

Signpost

Make the next step obvious.

Clear signs beat long explanations, which is why short state labels matter in my interfaces.

Adapt

Plans change mid-event.

Fallbacks, like AirSculpt’s mouse mode, keep an experience working when conditions aren’t perfect.

Identity

It shaped my Project 01 character.

My Four-Part Harmony character plays percussion and crowd sound and wears a headset, straight from event work.
